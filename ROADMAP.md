# RxSignal ideas to revisit after the core demo

## Proactive specialty notifications

Product goal: bring relevant medication safety updates to healthcare professionals
instead of relying on them to search. After sign-in, tailor updates to the user's
specialty and followed medications. Offer opt-in phone/browser push notifications
that open the relevant medication details, including when the dashboard is closed.

Revisit after authentication, specialty preferences, and the live alert feed exist.
Plan delivery timing, notification permission, subscriptions, duplicate prevention,
priority preferences, quiet hours, and read/unread state. Signing in alone does not
enable phone notifications; permission and delivery infrastructure are required.

## Healthcare professional community

Create a welcoming space for specialty groups to discuss discoveries, professional
experiences, and interesting facts. Consider discussion threads, replies, reactions,
and notifications. Keep professional discussion distinct from verified safety alerts.

Revisit toward the end, after the main medication workflow. Define identity,
community guidelines, moderation/reporting, and patient-privacy expectations before
launch. These are saved product ideas, not features implemented in the demo yet.
